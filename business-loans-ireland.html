Business Loans Ireland — FAQs

Common Business Finance Questions

What can a business loan be used for?

Depending on the finance product and lender criteria, businesses may seek funding for working capital, stock, equipment, expansion, vehicles, refurbishment, cash-flow requirements and other commercial purposes.

How much business finance can I apply for?

The amount available depends on factors such as the business, trading history, financial performance, funding purpose and the individual finance provider's criteria.

How quickly can business finance be approved?

Timescales vary by lender, finance product and the information required. FieldTech Finance helps organise the application process, but final approval and timing are determined by the finance provider.

Does FieldTech Finance provide the loan directly?

FieldTech Finance helps businesses explore funding routes through business finance providers. The lender or finance provider makes the final credit and funding decision.

Can I enquire without committing to a loan?

Yes. You can start with a finance enquiry to discuss the requirement and understand potential next steps before deciding whether to proceed with an application.
